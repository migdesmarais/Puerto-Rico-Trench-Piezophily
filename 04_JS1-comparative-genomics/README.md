# JS1/Atribacterota comparative genomics

Scripts used for comparative genomic analyses of JS1/Atribacterota genomes.

Analyses include functional annotation with KOfamScan, calculation of genome and predicted-proteome properties, comparison of genomic characteristics among phylogenetic and environmental groups, and ordination and statistical analysis of KEGG Ortholog (KO) profiles.

Genome inclusion criteria and statistical analyses follow those described in the manuscript.
